Quality Management System

A Quality Management System (QMS) is a centralized framework that helps organizations monitor, control, and improve product quality through standardized processes, inspections, and operational tracking.

QMS Core Modules

JIGS Management

The JIGS management module helps track jig availability, usage history, maintenance schedules, and operational status in the production environment.

  • Jig inventory tracking
  • Maintenance scheduling
  • Usage monitoring
  • Operational lifecycle management

JSIR – Job Setup Inspection Report

The JSIR module ensures that the production setup meets quality standards before starting manufacturing processes. It validates machine settings, tools, and setup conditions.

  • Setup inspection checklists
  • Process validation
  • Quality compliance tracking
  • Digital inspection records

PIR – Production Inspection Report

The PIR module captures inspection results during production to ensure product quality is maintained throughout manufacturing operations.

  • Production inspection reports
  • Quality deviation tracking
  • Inspection history records
  • Quality approval workflow

Material Monitor

The Material Monitor module tracks material consumption, inventory availability, and quality validation across production stages.

  • Material usage tracking
  • Inventory monitoring
  • Quality verification
  • Material traceability

Operations Management

The Operations module coordinates manufacturing activities, machine allocation, and production workflow to ensure smooth operational efficiency.

  • Production workflow control
  • Machine allocation
  • Process monitoring
  • Operational analytics
